## How to approve

1. Go through each table. Set `include` to `no`, or delete the row, to drop a candidate. Rows left at `yes` become the build input (§5.1 step 2).
2. Change `landmark` to `yes`/`no` as you see fit. The spec targets about 50.

4. Read the **Doubts** section at the end. It lists the rows to check before approving.

## Conventions

- **ids:** `ca-{ab|sb}-{number}-{year}` for California bills. The year is the year of final action (signed, vetoed or died), which keeps the spec's examples (`ca-sb-53-2025`, `ca-ab-2013-2024`) and separates reused numbers (e.g. the 2026 SB 1047 is an unrelated health bill). Other rows use `{jurisdiction}-{issuer}-{slug}-{year}`. A few rows whose date could not be confirmed have no year (see Doubts).
- **type:** bill, statute, regulation, executive-order, guidance, tender, press-release or hansard (spec §4). An AG legal advisory is `guidance`. An AG settlement or lawsuit announcement is `press-release`.
- **status:** starts with a spec §4 status word. The note in brackets is the best-known detail.
- **url_confidence:** `seen` means this exact URL appeared in a search result. `constructed` means it was built from a known pattern (leginfo `billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id={session}{house}{number}`, with session `202320240` or `202520260`, and the SSO `/Act/{code}` pattern). `seen (match unverified)` means the URL was seen but may not be the exact document described.
- Most Singapore **hansard** rows link to the transcript the ministry published (mddi.gov.sg, csa.gov.sg, mha.gov.sg), not to sprs.parl.gov.sg. Search surfaced only two sprs links.

### GeBIZ tender selection rule (replaces ~40 tender rows)

The build pulls tenders from data.gov.sg (spec §5.2). This research step could not query it, so the owner approves this rule, not rows.

- **Dataset:** "Government Procurement via GeBIZ", published by the Ministry of Finance under the Singapore Open Data Licence.
  - id **`d_acde1106003906a75c3fa052592f2fcb`**. Seen at https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_acde1106003906a75c3fa052592f2fcb/view.
  - Older landing page (seen): https://data.gov.sg/dataset/government-procurement.
  - Search snippets: about 18.5k rows of awarded open tenders from FY2021. The latest version covers Apr 2021 to Mar 2026 and was updated 2026-04-26.
  - Columns: `tender_no`, `tender_description`, `agency`, `award_date`, `tender_detail_status`, `supplier_name`, `awarded_amt`.
- **API (constructed, not verified):**
  - v2 download: `https://api-production.data.gov.sg/v2/public/api/datasets/d_acde1106003906a75c3fa052592f2fcb/poll-download`.
  - Legacy query: `https://data.gov.sg/api/action/datastore_search?resource_id=d_acde1106003906a75c3fa052592f2fcb`.
  - `api-production.data.gov.sg` must be allow-listed (spec §9).
- **Status filter:** keep `tender_detail_status` values meaning awarded (e.g. "Awarded to Suppliers", "Awarded by Items"). Drop "No award" and cancelled tenders.
- **Date range:** `award_date` from 2023-01-01 to the end of the dataset (currently 2026-03).
- **Value threshold:** `awarded_amt` of S$1,000,000 or more. If fewer than 40 rows qualify, lower it to S$500,000.
- **Keyword filter** (case-insensitive, whole words, on `tender_description`):
  - AI: `artificial intelligence`, `\bAI\b`, `machine learning`, `generative`, `large language model`, `LLM`, `chatbot`, `computer vision`, `video analytics`, `data analytics`, `data science`.
  - Cloud and data centres: `cloud`, `GCC`, `commercial cloud`, `SaaS`, `data centre`, `data center`, `co-location`, `hosting`, `server`.
  - Cybersecurity: `cyber`, `security operations centre`, `\bSOC\b`, `penetration test`, `vulnerability`, `managed security`, `threat`, `cybersecurity and audit`.
  - Digital infrastructure: `network`, `5G`, `broadband`, `fibre`, `submarine cable`, `Wi-Fi`.
- **Exclusions:** `security officer`, `security services` (guarding), `cleaning`, `manpower` unless an ICT keyword also matches, `CCTV maintenance` unless it also says analytics or AI.
- **Agency priority (tie-break, not a hard filter):** Government Technology Agency, Cyber Security Agency, IMDA, MDDI, Smart Nation Group, then any other agency.
- **Selection:** dedupe on `tender_no` and keep the top awards by `awarded_amt` within quotas: about 12 AI/analytics, 10 cloud/data-centre, 10 cybersecurity, and 8 digital-infrastructure or other gov-tech. That makes 40.
- **Record mapping:**
  - Fields: type `tender`, status `awarded`, topics from the matching keyword group plus `gov-tech-procurement`, landmark `no`.
  - id `sg-gebiz-{tender_no lowercased}`.
  - url: the dataset page plus the tender number. GeBIZ has no stable public deep link.
